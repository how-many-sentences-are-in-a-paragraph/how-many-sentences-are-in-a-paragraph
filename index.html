How Many Sentences Are in a Paragraph?

A paragraph usually contains 3–5 sentences, but there is no fixed rule.

What is a paragraph?

A paragraph is a group of sentences that explain one main idea or topic.

How many sentences should it contain?

  • Short paragraph: 1–2 sentences.
  • Medium paragraph: 3–5 sentences.
  • Long paragraph: 6 or more sentences.

Example

The sun rises in the east. Birds begin to sing. People start their day. The morning feels fresh.

This example has 4 sentences.
